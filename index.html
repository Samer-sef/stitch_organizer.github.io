Stitch Organizer

Keep the whole stitch stash in your pocket.

Stitch Organizer is a small companion app for cross-stitchers. It keeps a running inventory of your floss, plans each project thread-by-thread, and tells you exactly what to buy — so you never grab another skein of DMC 310 you already own.

  • Catalog your stash — every color and count, always at hand.
  • Plan projects — list required floss and track what's still missing.
  • Shop smart — build a list, then top up your stash in a tap.
  • Handy tools — fabric & floss calculators, color conversions, storage boxes.

Built by one stitcher, for stitchers. No ads, no accounts, no cloud.

Yours, and yours alone. Your projects, notes, and photos live only on your device.

Free on iPhone and Android
